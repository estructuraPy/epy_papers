# Introduction

Tall, slender towers are governed by wind rather than gravity. The
dynamic pressure the wind exerts on the facade scales with the square of
its speed,

$$ q = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho\,V^{2} $$

so gusts, not self-weight, size the lateral system.

# Bracing

The outrigger level ties the core to the perimeter columns and limits the
drift at the serviceability limit state.

# Conclusion

Wind governs the lateral design of this tower; the bracing system follows
from that demand.
